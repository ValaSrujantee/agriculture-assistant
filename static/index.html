Agricultural Cropping Seasons & Farm Activity Schedule

Standard farming cycles, sowing windows, and harvest management.

Monsoon Cycle

Kharif Season

📅 Sowing: June - July | Harvest: Sept - Oct

Relies heavily on southwest monsoon rainfall. High humidity and warm temperatures facilitate fast vegetative growth.

Key Crops:
Rice (Paddy) Cotton Maize Soybean
Winter Cycle

Rabi Season

📅 Sowing: Oct - Dec | Harvest: Mar - April

Crops require cool climate during germination and vegetative growth, followed by warm sunshine during ripening.

Key Crops:
Wheat Chickpea (Gram) Potato Mustard
Summer Cycle

Zaid Season

📅 Sowing: Mar - April | Harvest: May - June

Short duration interim season between Rabi and Kharif. Suitable for fast-maturing crops with artificial irrigation.

Key Crops:
Watermelon Muskmelon Cucumber Fodder
